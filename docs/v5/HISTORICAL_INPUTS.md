# Historical handoff inputs

`BASELINE.json` is the immutable v4 source/archive and acceptance snapshot supplied with the v5 handoff. Its statuses, artifact hashes and human-review notes describe that earlier capture. Its unchanged bytes also anchor the v5 archive/integration proofs. It is not the current v5 acceptance inventory.

`V4_TO_V5_MAP.json`, `V4_REVIEW.md` and the research snapshots preserve the supplied task mapping, source provenance and observation limits. Preparation output is test/research evidence, not a claim that the current production app, physical devices or human motion review passed.

The complete review ZIP's canonical `review/acceptance.json` and matching publication receipt describe the final v5 release. Current production, native, visual, fixture and human scope remain separate there. Historical input statuses must not be substituted for those final rows.

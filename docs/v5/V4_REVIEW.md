## Guest qualification finding

Two aggregate attempts failed before triggering their watchdog scenarios. The
diagnostic separately reached controller readiness at 15.158 seconds and workers
at 48.810/48.896 seconds. The standalone deadline terminated all three within
2.300 seconds with cleanup. These measurements demonstrate a discrepancy, not its
cause. `native/supervisor.py` uses bounded repeated readiness requests and the
aggregate probe provisions from a different lifecycle context. Add at most one
targeted sanitized phase/health trace if existing logs do not distinguish the
cause, compare identical source/boot/resource conditions, and repair the actual
initialization/lifecycle issue. Do not guess that increasing timeouts is a fix,
copy an old qualification receipt, or bypass the gate to render fake task success.

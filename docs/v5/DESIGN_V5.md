## Composition

Use the exact [Awsmd dashboard](https://dribbble.com/shots/21673507-Task-Management-Dashboard)
for the outer shell, inset navigation, neutral translucent material and spacing.
Use [Linear's March 2026 refresh](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh)
for hierarchy, subdued rail text, compact rows and stable header roles inside
that shell. Both were inspected in the research. They do not prescribe Hermes
content or authorize copying unrelated imagery.

The candidate neutralizes the existing tokens, reduces outer gap to 12px and
uses 22px shell radii. It gives the selected rail item a clear tonal inversion,
reveals secondary row actions on hover/focus, reduces the composer padding and
removes redundant model-state text. These are independently authored starting
values. Refine them against real populated app output, not against invented art.
Keep pointer targets, keyboard access, forced colors and text contrast intact.

## Motion plan

Keep the portfolio buddy's bounded cursor tracking, smoothing, sleep, drag cancel,
theme states and visibility cleanup. It is already implemented; do not replace it
with a new mascot. Keep the accepted 12% click pair, mute and exactly one cue per
accepted action. Do not add cursor-follow sounds.

Frequently used header popovers should feel direct. Preserve the current maximum
230ms panel transition as a ceiling while testing shorter response; reduced
motion removes travel/scale. Review interrupted open/close, switching between
panels, Escape and repeated activation. Never animate layout width/padding of the
header to achieve an effect. Trigger drift must stay within the proposed 1 CSS
pixel regression threshold. A neutral static material is the fallback when
transparency is reduced, forced colors are used, or GPU contention makes an
effect expensive.

Record source-to-implementation geometry, appearance, input, semantics and motion
separately. Do not call the whole app “1:1” when runtime capability or content
necessarily differs. Preserve the requested visual core, document each concrete
adaptation, and leave approval pending until Mani reviews actual output.

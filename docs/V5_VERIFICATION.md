compared against a private backup in read-only mode and remained present. Test
records are positively identified; the catalog is never restored over later data.
Normal configuration, accepted click bytes, launcher and protected controls are
checked separately from UI evidence.

Earlier aggregate boot failures and a separate standalone deadline pass do not
establish a cause or all-five qualification. The bounded readiness observer keeps
the original budgets and requires native authentication. No host fallback,
expanded timeout, copied receipt or self-issued qualification is permitted.

Exact R01–R11 references remain locked. Source code integrity, independently
authored adaptations, rendered interactions and subjective parity are different
claims. R10's canonical original/designer remains uncertain; a related Pinterest
pin is not relabelled as proof. Source clips and third-party comparison artwork
stay private. Integrated RareUI notices and visible attribution remain included.

assistive technology and a separately coordinated reboot remain unverified unless
the final acceptance row has explicit evidence. A build, screenshot, fixture or
unreviewed recording cannot grant that approval. The complete review retains
blocked and partial requirements instead of declaring the whole project finished.
